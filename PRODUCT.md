# Contech DC Dashboard

## Product focus

Contech DC Dashboard is the local document-control workspace for construction
projects. It gives Engineers, Document Controllers, Department Heads, and
Administrators a controlled view of submissions, reviews, project records, and
linked revisions.

## Primary users

- **Engineers** submit and follow their assigned document requests.
- **Document Controllers** maintain the official document workflow and records.
- **Department Heads** review department-level work.
- **Administrators** provision accounts, set roles, and maintain configuration.

## Experience goals

- Make the next safe action obvious without hiding important context.
- Keep controlled data, status, ownership, and linked records easy to scan.
- Treat sign-in as a quiet, dependable gateway; public account registration is
  intentionally unavailable.
- Put administrative setup actions, including account creation, in Admin Mode.

## Constraints

- The application runs locally during development.
- Account roles and request types are controlled values, not free-form input.
- Revisions must remain connected to their parent document workflow.
